Pilot Canvas

Each group fills this single page in 30 minutes: problem, baseline, scope, roles, success criteria, risks, and the day-90 decision.

The ten canvas cells

1. Problem & user

Whose pain, for whom? Where does the user stand in the process?

2. Current state & baseline

How is it done today? What is today's number?

3. Scope & exclusions

Where exactly does the pilot sit? What is explicitly out?

4. Data & owner

What data is needed? Who owns and authorizes it?

5. AI role, rules & humans

What does the AI do and not do? Who holds the final decision?

6. Tools & connections

Which tools, connected to which systems, read-only or write?

7. Evaluation samples

With which real (de-identified) cases do we measure quality?

8. Success criteria

Which number, defined how, by when? (quality/time/adoption/risk/economics)

9. Risks & controls

If it breaks, what happens? What is the control and the rollback path?

10. Pilot owner & day-90 decision

Who is accountable? With what evidence do we decide on day 90: scale, fix, or stop?

Ten test samples

Five “should answer well” samples (common, high-frequency cases) and five “should correctly decline or refer” samples (out of scope, ambiguous, incomplete, or sensitive). Do not use real personal data; de-identify every sample.

30 / 60 / 90-day checklist

  1. Days 1–30: pick the scope, measure the baseline, secure data access and its owner, build test samples, define controls.
  2. Days 31–60: build the limited prototype, evaluate with experts against the test samples, fix errors and rules.
  3. Days 61–90: controlled rollout with real users, measure usage and criteria, make the day-90 decision: scale, fix, or stop.
