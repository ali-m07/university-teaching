Opportunity Map Worksheet

List the recurring tasks in your unit, define the AI role and the human reviewer, then prioritize.
How to fill it inEach row is a “task,” not a job. Write tasks that recur monthly and have a clear input and output. The highlighted row is your worked example.
Keep the AI role narrowFor each task pick one primary role: structuring input, knowledge search, drafting, routing suggestions, or limited action with human confirmation.
Priority score (4–20)Sum four 1–5 criteria: monthly frequency + time per instance + fit of the AI role + data access readiness. A score of 16+ makes a good pilot candidate; a low score means “not yet.”
Impact of errorIf the AI gets this task wrong, what happens? “Low” (internal text), “medium” (customer-facing reply), “high” (hiring, legal, or data-access decisions). High impact always requires a human reviewer.
